### Different Types of Healers in Ubud
Before choosing a healer, it helps to understand the different types of healing available in Ubud.
#### 1. Traditional Balinese Healers
Traditional Balinese healers, often called **Balian**, are part of Bali’s spiritual and cultural landscape. Some work with prayer, intuition, bodywork, herbs, energy, ritual, or spiritual guidance. Many visitors are drawn to traditional Balinese healing because it feels connected to the island’s deeper cultural roots.
If you are looking for a **Balinese healer in Ubud**, it is important to approach the experience respectfully. This is not just a tourist activity. It is a cultural and spiritual practice, and each healer will work differently.
A good guide, local recommendation, or trusted wellness centre can help you find someone appropriate.
#### 2. Energy Healers in Ubud
Energy healing is one of the most popular forms of healing in Ubud. An **energy healer in Ubud** may work with the body’s subtle energy system, emotional patterns, chakras, intuitive awareness, or spiritual connection.
People often choose energy healing when they feel blocked, heavy, disconnected, emotionally overwhelmed, or ready to release old patterns.
A good energy healing session should feel safe, respectful, and grounded. You should not feel pressured, judged, or told who you are. The session should support you to reconnect with your own inner awareness.
#### 3. Chakra Healing in Ubud
Many people search for **chakra healing in Ubud** when they feel emotionally or energetically out of balance. Chakra healing usually focuses on the body’s energy centres and may include meditation, intuitive guidance, sound, breath, visualisation, or hands-on energy work.
People may seek chakra healing for support with grounding, emotional openness, confidence, communication, intuition, or spiritual connection.
As with all healing work, it is important to choose a practitioner who does not overpromise. Chakra healing can be a powerful reflective and energetic experience, but it should not be presented as a guaranteed cure for medical or psychological conditions.
#### 4. Sound Healing in Ubud
Sound healing is another popular healing experience in Bali. A **sound healing session in Ubud** may use crystal bowls, gongs, voice, drums, chimes, or other instruments to create a deeply meditative state.
Many people enjoy sound healing because it does not require much talking. It can be a gentle way to relax the nervous system, quiet the mind, and create space for reflection.
Sound healing may be offered in group sessions, private sessions, retreats, or ceremony-style experiences.
#### 5. Breathwork and Somatic Healing
Breathwork and somatic healing are often chosen by people who want to reconnect with the body, release stored tension, or move through emotional patterns. These sessions can be powerful, so it is important to work with someone properly trained, trauma-aware, and clear about safety.
A good practitioner will explain the process, check your comfort level, discuss any contraindications, and allow you to pause or stop at any time.
#### 6. Hypnotherapy and Subconscious Work
Some healing centres in Ubud offer hypnotherapy or subconscious reprogramming. These sessions may support people who want to explore habits, beliefs, fears, emotional loops, or inner conflict.
Hypnotherapy should always be consent-based, respectful, and collaborative. You should feel aware, safe, and in control throughout the process.
#### 7. Private Healing Retreats in Ubud
For people seeking deeper support, a **private healing retreat in Ubud** may be more suitable than a single session. A retreat can give you time to slow down, reflect, receive multiple forms of support, and integrate what arises.
A good retreat should not overwhelm you with too many activities. The best healing retreats often include spaciousness, rest, reflection, nature, nourishing food, skilled practitioners, and time for integration.
### How to Choose the Best Healer in Ubud for You
When people ask, “Who are the best healers in Ubud?” the better question may be:
**Who is the right healer for what I need right now?**
Here are some things to consider.
#### 1. Look for Safety, Not Just Spiritual Language
A healer may use beautiful words, but what matters most is how they hold space.
Look for someone who is clear, calm, respectful, and grounded. A good healer should explain what they offer, answer your questions, respect your boundaries, and avoid making dramatic claims.
Be cautious of anyone who says they can instantly fix your life, remove all your problems, diagnose you spiritually, or guarantee a specific outcome.
#### 2. Choose Someone Who Respects Your Sovereignty
The best healers do not make you dependent on them. They help you reconnect with yourself.
A good healing session should leave you feeling more aware, more centred, and more able to listen to your own inner guidance. It should not make you feel powerless, labelled, or afraid.
#### 3. Check the Type of Healing Offered
Different healers work in different ways. Before booking, ask yourself what you are actually looking for.
Do you want emotional release?  
Do you want spiritual insight?  
Do you want relaxation?  
Do you want help understanding a pattern?  
Do you want a traditional Balinese healing experience?  
Do you want private support rather than a group session?  
Do you want a full retreat rather than one appointment?
When you are clear on your intention, it becomes easier to choose the right practitioner.
#### 4. Read the Language Carefully
A trustworthy healer or healing centre will usually be careful with their language. They may speak about support, reflection, transformation, energy, reconnection, release, or personal growth.
Be cautious with language that promises guaranteed healing, instant cures, or supernatural certainty.
Healing is personal. It can be powerful, but it should also be honest.
#### 5. Trust How You Feel Before the Session
Before booking, notice how you feel when reading the website, messaging the practitioner, or walking into the space.
Do you feel calm?  
Do you feel respected?  
Do you feel pressured?  
Do you feel safe to ask questions?  
Do you feel the practitioner is listening?
Your body often gives you useful information.
### Questions to Ask Before Booking a Healing Session in Ubud
Before choosing a healer in Ubud, you may want to ask:
What type of healing do you offer?  
What usually happens during a session?  
How long does the session last?  
Is there anything I need to prepare?  
Are there any reasons someone should not do this session?  
Can I stop or pause the session if I feel uncomfortable?  
Do you offer integration support afterwards?  
Is this a private session or a group experience?  
What should I realistically expect?
A good practitioner will welcome thoughtful questions.
### What to Avoid When Looking for Healers in Ubud
Ubud has many sincere and talented practitioners, but not every experience will be right for every person.
Be careful with any healer who:
Promises to cure serious conditions  
Creates fear or dependency  
Tells you that you are broken  
Uses pressure-based sales tactics  
Claims only they can help you  
Discourages medical or psychological support when needed  
Pushes you into emotional intensity without proper care  
Does not explain consent, boundaries, or process  
Makes you feel judged, exposed, or unsafe
The best healing experiences are not built on fear. They are built on trust.
