Stress is the constant application of ‘Force’ in our lives.  To experience greater ease and flow in your life, what can you surrender to today?
[IMAGE] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/55cd8826e4b0af9801ddffe4/1454040834319-T93TWB56TV9ZSCBJJVH0/image-asset.png (720x405)
- If you get stuck in traffic, can you relax and breathe?
- If a task takes longer than your ego would like you to complete it, can you relax and breathe?
- If you encounter a grumpy person and you feel that you do not deserve their reaction, can you relax and breathe?
- If your computer decides to have a mind of its own, can you relax and breathe?
- If you wake up expecting your day to be one way and it turns out that you are required to live very differently that day, can you relax and breathe?
Do any of these things really matter? We each have a choice; we can either push and control or surrender and breathe.  Choose to surrender instead.    The 50 trillion cells in your body will thank-you.
Have a wonderful and Juicy Effect kind of day!!!
