#### How Does Trust Feel in Your Body?
One of the themes that often arises during **Shamanic Energy Healing sessions** is the desire to surrender, let go, and trust life more deeply. Clients frequently tell me that they want to stop fighting life. They want to trust the unfolding. They want to feel guided rather than controlled by fear. They want to move through uncertainty with greater peace and confidence.
‍  ‍
To me, this is one of the great mastery journeys of being human. Not because life becomes easier, but because we learn to meet life differently. We learn to soften our grip. We learn to release the illusion that we can control every outcome. We learn to trust ourselves, trust our soul, and trust the deeper intelligence moving through life.
‍  ‍
When a client expresses this desire, I know they are being called into a fuller expression of who they came here to be. Because becoming more of who we truly are requires a degree of surrender. It requires releasing old identities. It requires stepping beyond certainty. It requires saying yes to life even when we cannot see the entire path ahead.
‍  ‍
Rather than asking yourself, "Do I trust life?" try asking a different question: **How would trust feel in my body?**
‍  ‍
Pause for a moment. Take a breath. Imagine that everything currently unfolding in your life was being held within a larger intelligence. The joyful parts. The beautiful parts. The challenging parts. The heartbreaking parts. The uncertain parts. The parts that make no sense at all.
‍  ‍
*What would it feel like if your body met all of it with a deep and trusting yes?*
‍  ‍
Not necessarily approval. Not necessarily liking what is happening. But an openness to life as it is.  Would your shoulders soften? Would your breath deepen? Would your heart open? Would your nervous system relax? Would there be more spaciousness within you?
‍  ‍
Trust is not simply a thought. Trust is an embodied experience. It is something we feel. And often the body reveals where trust has not yet been established.
‍  ‍
Many people believe they should trust more. Yet beneath the surface may live experiences that taught them otherwise. Past disappointments. Betrayals. Loss. Unresolved grief. Childhood conditioning. Ancestral patterns. Generational survival strategies. Deep unconscious beliefs.
‍  ‍
From a shamanic perspective, we can also explore karmic imprints, soul contracts, energetic entanglements, and inherited patterns that influence how we move through life. These layers often operate beneath conscious awareness. They shape our choices. They influence our relationships. They affect our ability to receive. And they can limit our capacity to trust the flow of life itself.
‍  ‍
In a Shamanic Energy Healing session we are not attempting to force surrender. We are creating a space where the barriers to surrender can begin to reveal themselves. The unconscious patterns. The inherited beliefs. The ancestral imprints. The energetic attachments. The outdated survival mechanisms. The parts of you that still believe it is safer to control than to trust.
‍  ‍
When these layers begin to emerge into awareness, they can be witnessed, understood, and gradually unravelled.
‍  ‍
This is why healing is often a journey rather than a single event. Each layer that softens creates access to a deeper level of trust. Each session invites you into a greater degree of surrender that is appropriate for your life, your nervous system, and your soul's current stage of evolution.
‍  ‍
One of the reasons I love this work is that it continually reminds me how multidimensional we truly are. We are more than our thoughts. More than our stories. More than our current circumstances. Within each person exists a vast landscape of experiences, wisdom, gifts, memories, and possibilities waiting to be explored.
‍  ‍
Every healing session reveals another layer. Another doorway. Another opportunity for remembrance. Another invitation into greater wholeness.
‍  ‍
The healing journey is not about becoming someone else. It is about uncovering more of who you have always been.
‍  ‍
Take a moment and place your hand on your heart. Ask yourself:   **"If I trusted life just 5% more than I do right now, how would that feel in my body?"**
‍  ‍
Notice what changes. Notice what softens. Notice what opens. Allow yourself to experience that feeling, even for a few moments.  Because every journey into trust begins with a single yes.  And perhaps that yes is not to a future outcome.  Perhaps it is simply a yes to this moment.
‍  ‍
A yes to your healing.
‍  ‍
A yes to your unfolding.
‍  ‍
A yes to life itself.
