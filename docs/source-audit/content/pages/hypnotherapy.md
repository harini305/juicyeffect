### What is Hypnotherapy?
Hypnotherapy is a highly effective modality for accessing the unconscious mind.   The unconscious mind stores our memories, our habits and also the beliefs that we hold about ourselves and the world around us.   These beliefs are like a computer program and the unconscious mind works to make them a reality in our day-to-day experience.  Neuroscience has shown that most of our decisions, actions, emotions and behaviours depend on the 95 percent of brain activity that lies beyond conscious awareness, meaning that 95 percent of your life comes from the programming in your unconscious mind.
During a hypnotherapy session you will be guided into a Theta brain wave state (a state of deep relaxation).  When in this brainwave frequency the door to the unconscious mind is open and limiting or sabotaging beliefs can be replaced with those that will support you in creating the life that you desire.
#### Programming of the unconscious mind occurs before age Six
The predominant brain activity during a child’s first two years of life is Delta, which is the lowest brain-wave frequency range. Between two and six years of age, a child’s brain activity increases and operates primarily in the range of Theta. While in this Theta state, children spend much of their time moving between the imaginary world with the real world. Delta and Theta brain frequencies define a brain state known as a Trance—the same brainwave state that hypnotherapists use to directly download new beliefs into the unconscious minds of their clients.
What this means is that the first 6 years of a child’s life are largely spent in a state of hypnotic trance!  During this time a child is downloading the opinions and beliefs of those around them about themselves and the world -  largely without a filter.  Is it any wonder that when we reach adulthood we find that many of the these beliefs do not serve us very well or in some cases are limiting or sabotaging.
