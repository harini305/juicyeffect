Known for her grounding and nurturing presence, Vickya intuitively guides each session based on what the individual may need in the moment.
Through sound, vibration, and intuitive guidance, she creates a deeply immersive experience designed to help release tension, restore balance, and cultivate a greater sense of clarity and inner peace. Her intention is simple — to create a safe space where people can slow down, reconnect with themselves, and feel more at home within their own body and energy.
