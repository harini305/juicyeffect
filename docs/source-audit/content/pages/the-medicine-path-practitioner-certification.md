### Certification Requirements
To receive certification as a Shamanic Ceremonial Practitioner through The Juicy Effect, participants must:
- Attend and complete all training sessions
- Demonstrate competency in journeying (Lower World, Upper World, and for others)
- Demonstrate competency in core practices (power animal work, cord cutting, soul retrieval)
- Successfully complete a number of documented practice sessions with real clients
- Submit case studies demonstrating understanding, integration, and application of the work
- Show ability to hold safe, grounded, and ethical space
- Demonstrate strong energetic boundaries and self-regulation
- Integrate feedback and show growth throughout the training
Certification is based not only on knowledge, but on embodiment, integrity, and readiness to work with others.
### Certification Outcome
Upon successful completion, you will be certified by The Juicy Effect as a Shamanic Ceremonial Practitioner.  This reflects your ability to work with clients safely, ethically, and with grounded presence.
