## Section 2  `theme=white`
There are moments in life when something within us knows that we cannot continue exactly as we have before.  
  
Perhaps you are moving through a significant transition. Perhaps you have spent years caring for others, holding everything together, giving your energy away, or living according to expectations that no longer feel like your own. Perhaps you simply know that a deeper part of you is ready to emerge.  
  
**The Womb Sovereign**is an invitation to step away from the noise of everyday life and return to yourself.
This is not a group retreat. There is no packed schedule, no need to perform your transformation in front of other people, and no expectation that every moment of your day should be filled.  
  
This is a private, one-to-one experience created around you — your needs, your intentions, your energetic landscape and the woman you are becoming.
Held in the beautiful surroundings of Ubud, Bali, you will stay in an exclusive private villa on site and be personally guided through a transformational journey centred around the Divine Feminine, the Womb, feminine wisdom, protection, self-trust, inner authority and sovereignty.
At the heart of the retreat is a three-part Womb Healing journey: Womb of the Ancients, Meeting the Womb Guardian, and Embodying the Inner Queen. Each journey combines a guided Shamanic Vision Quest with a hands-on energy healing session. The journeys are intentionally prepared for through somatic practices and guided inner work, and followed by dedicated journal prompts and spacious integration time.
The retreat also includes a separate Shamanic Energy Healing session, private mentoring with Sharon, nourishing vegetarian and gluten-free breakfast and lunch from Unity Cafe, two massages in the privacy of your villa, an opening ceremony, a closing ceremony, time in nature and generous space to rest, reflect and simply be.
The intention is not to give you more to do. It is to create enough space for you to listen deeply — to your body, your intuition, your womb, your inner knowing and the part of you that may already know what is ready to change.
#### The Transformational Arc
1. OPEN — arriving, crossing the threshold and setting your intention.
2. RETURN — reconnecting with the Womb through the Womb of the Ancients.
3. PROTECT — meeting the Womb Guardian and exploring boundaries, discernment and embodied safety.
4. RECLAIM — reconnecting with the Inner Queen and the capacity to choose, lead and trust yourself.
5. EMBODY — integrating the work and translating the experience into practical tools for life at home.
#### Womb Preparation & Inner Practice
Womb Preparation & Inner Practice is an essential part of the retreat rather than an add-on. The day before each major journey, you will meet privately with Sharon for a dedicated preparation session. This becomes your inner-work time: a guided space to come into the body, explore somatic awareness, connect with the aspect of the Womb you are preparing to meet, clarify your intention and receive journal prompts to work with afterwards.
- Somatic practices and grounding
- Womb and heart connection
- Exploring how you naturally sense inner experiences — feeling, imagery, sound and knowing
- Specific preparation for the Womb of the Ancients, Womb Guardian and Inner Queen
- Embodied boundary and yes/no awareness
- Inner authority and embodied Queen practices
- Personal intention setting
- Journal prompts to explore privately before the next journey
After each journey, you will receive dedicated journal prompts to explore what you experienced, what shifted, what was revealed and what is asking to be integrated. The remainder of the day is intentionally spacious so that you can rest, journal, walk, sleep, reflect or simply allow the work to settle.
#### The Three Womb Journeys
Each Womb journey follows the same core structure:
- Womb Preparation & Inner Practice — somatic preparation, orientation, intention and journalling.
- Shamanic Vision Quest — a guided journey to meet the specific aspect of Womb intelligence being explored.
- Hands-on Energy Healing — energy healing to support integration, anchoring and stabilisation of what has opened.
- Post-Journey Integration — dedicated journal prompts followed by spacious time for rest and reflection.
The three journeys are Womb of the Ancients, Meeting the Womb Guardian and Embodying the Inner Queen.
1 - The Womb
In Womb Alchemy, the womb is approached as a multidimensional portal and a centre of feminine power, sovereignty, intuition, creation and receiving. The work honours both the natural power of the womb and the experiences that may have been held there — including learned emotional patterns, relationship experiences, breaches of boundaries, grief, fear, shame, guilt and experiences of disempowerment, as well as what may have travelled through the ancestral line.
2 - The Womb Guardian
The Womb Guardian represents the part of your energetic blueprint that knows what feels safe and what does not. She stands at the threshold. She senses what is allowed in, when to open, when to soften and when to hold a boundary. The work is not about getting rid of protection, but about reconnecting with this intelligence and restoring a clearer, more balanced relationship with your own yes and no.
3 - The Inner Queen
The Inner Queen is not something you become; she is something you remember. She is a state of embodied self-trust — a way of leading your life from grounded inner knowing rather than pressure, proving or the need for approval. Where the Guardian governs access, the Queen governs direction. The Guardian stands at the gate; the Queen sits on the throne.

The schedule is intentionally spacious. The aim is not to fill every moment, but to create a rhythm in which the woman is guided deeply when she is with Sharon, and then given meaningful time to do her own inner work, journal, rest, reflect and allow each stage of the journey to integrate.
The 3:00pm Womb Preparation & Inner Practice sessions are an essential part of this rhythm. They create the bridge between one day's experience and the next threshold, so that each journey is approached consciously and given time to unfold within her.
