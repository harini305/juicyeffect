Gita is a certified Yoga and Rebirthing Breathwork Facilitator. She first encountered Rebirthing Breathwork whilst teaching Yoga in Sydney, Australia, helping her to overcome and heal her own old wounds.  Yoga teaching was especially difficult during the pandemic in Australia, and she credits this breathwork technique for helping her get through and now specialises in helping others to experience the journey of self-discovering and acceptance that Rebirthing Breathwork can provide.
Now living in Bali, Gita helps people from all over the world to dive deeper and witness the benefits this healing modality brings.
She also has qualifications in Reiki and is gifted in the art of sound healing.
