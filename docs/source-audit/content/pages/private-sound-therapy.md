#### About this session
**Private Sound Therapy** is an intimate 1-to-1 experience designed to calm the nervous system, release energetic tension, and bring the body and mind into a state of deeper harmony.
Each session is intuitively guided and tailored to what your body and energy may need in the moment, creating a deeply immersive experience for rest, restoration, and inner connection.
A signature element of the session is the placement of singing bowls directly on and around the body, working through selected chakra and meridian points across both the front and back of the body. The gentle vibrations create a deeply immersive sensory experience, allowing you to connect more deeply with your physical body while supporting relaxation, grounding, and energetic balance.
This experience may be especially supportive for those navigating stress, emotional overwhelm, mental fatigue, difficulty relaxing, energetic heaviness, or simply feeling the need to pause and reconnect with themselves.
The experience is designed to support:
- Deep relaxation and nervous system regulation
- A shift toward slower, more restful brainwave states
- Parasympathetic nervous system activation
- Encouraging the body’s natural rest-and-digest response
- Release of emotional and physical tension
- Energetic balance and restoration
- Deeper rest and improved sleep
- Reconnection with self and emotional awareness
An optional consultation is available at the beginning or end of the session, should you wish to share what you are experiencing, discuss an intention for your session, or reflect on your experience afterwards.
Come as you are.   Simply rest, receive, and allow the sound to guide you inward.
