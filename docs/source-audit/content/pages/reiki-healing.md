### What to expect from this session?
Your Reiki session begins with a gentle consultation, where we take a few moments to connect, discuss any intentions, and explore what you’re currently experiencing—physically, emotionally, or energetically. This space is entirely supportive and confidential, allowing you to feel safe, heard, and seen.
You’ll then lie down comfortably, fully clothed, while our Reiki Master Teacher channels healing energy.  Hands will be lightly placed on or just above your body, serving as a bridge for the high vibrational frequencies of healing to flow exactly where they are needed—addressing body, mind, and spirit across all levels.
Each session is unique and intuitively guided, often experienced as deeply calming, heart-opening, or emotionally clarifying. You may feel warmth, tingling, lightness, or even see colours or receive inner guidance during the healing.
The session closes with a short integration space, offering time for gentle grounding and optional reflection. You are welcome to share your experience, ask questions, or simply sit in silence as your system continues to receive and settle the angelic frequencies. Guidance and suggestions for post-session self-care may also be offered to support your continued integration.
