Here’s the answers to other frequently asked questions…
**Is Womb Alchemy right for me if I have not done deep healing work before?**
Yes. Womb Alchemy is held with reverence and care, and the pacing across three months gives your nervous system the room it needs to adjust. The preparation videos and the gentle structure of each session create a safe entry point if this is new territory. If you are already an experienced practitioner, the work goes as deep as you allow it to.
**What if I cannot make a scheduled session date?**
All three sessions are scheduled together at the start, with flexibility built in. If life shifts, we will reschedule. This journey is yours to receive in your own timing, not on a rigid calendar.
**I am not in Bali. Is the energy healing as effective at a distance?**
Yes. All three sessions are delivered online and the energy healing component works through resonance rather than physical proximity. Distance healing has a long lineage in shamanic and energetic traditions. Many of our most profound transformations have come from women on the other side of the world.
**How is this different from therapy?**
Therapy works primarily through cognition and language. Womb Alchemy works through ceremony, somatic awareness, ancestral resonance and energetic clearing. The two complement each other beautifully. This work is not a replacement for mental health care, particularly if you are currently in active crisis, but it reaches places that talk based modalities often cannot.
**What support do I have between sessions?**
After each live session you receive a personalised voice note transmission with reflections, integration guidance and energetic support. Before each session, a preparation video grounds you in somatic practices that open and steady the womb space. You are not left alone between initiations.
**Can I do this if I am pregnant, postpartum, in menopause, or have had a hysterectomy?**
Yes. The womb is an energetic space that exists regardless of your physical reproductive status, and we adapt the work depending on where you are in your cycle of life. If you have specific medical or somatic concerns, please reach out before enrolling so we can talk it through.
