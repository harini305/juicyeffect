## Section 2  `theme=white-bold`
We are really lucky here at The Juicy Effect.  We have an amazing property with plenty of space, which provides us with an opportunity to offer this additional space to like-minded people to rent when required.  Whether it’s a 1:1 treatment room for a few hours, an event space for a day or a training space for a few weeks, we have options available.
We even have people who have taken a full-time space in our property, under their own brand, in order to share their gifts with the world.
if you are looking for a private, unique place in Ubud to impress your customers at a reasonable price, we might just have the space you are looking for.

**For up to two people**, we have our intimate treatment space overlooking the jungle that you can rent by the hour, half day or full day.  Here we have two massage tables that can be moved as required, a mattress and a desk, with it’s own bathroom and tea/coffee making facilities.  There is even a table and chairs overlooking the jungle for private consultations.
[IMAGE] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/55cd8826e4b0af9801ddffe4/908750bb-0965-4481-b0a7-5d29e9b04211/Event+space+for+rent+in+Ubud+at+The+Juicy+Effect (1280x960) — alt: "Event space for rent in Ubud at The Juicy Effect"
**For events and trainings**, we have our larger space that can hold up to 15 people comfortably (or more if it is a dance-style event), and has a bathroom, fridge and tea/coffee making.  There are yoga mats, blankets, blocks and cushions available, and if you need a projector/screen, no problem, we have those too.  There is no branding in this building, which means you can offer your event and training under your own brand without any confusion with ours.
There is ample partking, reception, and our own staff to help you impress your customers.   You can keep out those pesky mosquitos during raining season by closing the windows and turning on the fans and air conditioning, or if you prefer to keep the windows open and be closer to nature and the sound of the waterfall in the distance, no problem.
And with Unity Cafe, one of Ubud’s highest rating cafe’s located on the same property, your customers can enjoy amazing food and drinks without having to leave the property.
Most importantly, we have the right operating license for you to run events.  Prices start from just 150k per hour depending on your requirements.
Simply [contact us](/contact)to discuss your requirements and we will see if you can help you.
