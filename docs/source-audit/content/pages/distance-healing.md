### **Preparation for the session**
It is important that you set an intention(s) for the session and consider the outcome that you desire.   You will always receive more than this, but it sets up an openness and resonance in your field to accept the healing.   So, prior to the session please consider deeply:
1. What you would like to let go of, and
2. What you would like to call into your life.
We can discuss these more when you are booking in for your session.
Sharon spends approximately 30 to 40 minutes preparing for a session which involves a shamanic practice of opening sacred space.   It is important that you start to slow down at least 30 minutes before the session.  It is hard to be receptive to healing energy when we are in a busy go-go or Beta brain-wave state’.   You might like consider playing some music and thinking about your intentions.
At the start of the session Sharon will call you to discuss your intentions and what has been showing up for you (over Zoom or Whatsapp). Then she will guide you through a meditation to ground you back into your body.  It is important that you can remain in a quiet, undisturbed place for your session, remaining open to receiving the healing energy.    Following this, Sharon will hang up the phone and the energy healing component of the session begins. She will gain permission to access your subtle body and energy field.  At the end of the session she will call you back to discuss the session.
A typical session is approximately **90 - 100 minutes.**
