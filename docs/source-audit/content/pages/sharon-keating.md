Sharon is a compassionate, intuitive healer and teacher with a wealth of experience and expertise across a range of healing disciplines.   Working from a deep state of connection to the subtle energetic world, this frequency supports her clients in connecting back to their True Selves, releasing trauma, limiting beliefs, accessing their power and discovering their own unique expression of their gifts.    Her compassionate and supportive nature allows her to create a deeply supportive and caring space where her clients can drop into a deep state of healing.
With 10+ years experience in the health and wellbeing industry, she is a Certified Shamanic Practitioner (Diploma: Shaman College Circle of Earth and Spirit), certified Hypnotherapist, certified Kinesiology Practitioner, certified Ayurvedic Lifestyle Consultant and Body-Mind Nutritionist and Yoga Nidra Instructor. She also has expertise in a range of other energy-related disciplines including Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy and Vibrational Chakra Balancing.
Sharon understands the demands of living in the modern world, having had senior level positions in Global companies for 15 years (certified Change Management Practitioner, First Class Honours Bachelor of Commerce/Master of Commerce).   Her approach is to take ancient wisdom and apply it in a way that is accessible for modern world living.   Her belief is that this is the key to healing – *“we need to be able to walk in the modern world in a way that is healthy and sustainable and allows us to express our gifts in a way that honours our unique energetic blueprint.  To do this we need to shed our conditioning and all of the ways that we have learned to limit ourselves and put ourselves in boxes so that we can embrace our authentic soul led nature.”*
Sharon’s passion for teaching began as a Lecturer at a renowned university in Australia (Curtin University).  Today she runs transformational workshops around the world.  She teaches from a place of soul-led wisdom and shares knowledge in way that allows participants to assimilate it and create real positive change in their lives.
