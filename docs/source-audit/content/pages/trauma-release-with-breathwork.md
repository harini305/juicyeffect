### About this session
Various wisdom traditions honour the direct connection between the subconscious mind and the breath.   **Rebirthing Breathwork** takes you on a journey to explore this connection, to tap into your inner world, and to release unnecessary blocks that are having unwanted impacts.
This 120-minute private session starts with a guided meditation for self check-in and intention setting, transitioning into our guided circular breathing technique for 60 minutes.  This is all about building energy flow throughout the body and mind which you will feel both physically (tingling is common) and energetically.  We finish with a Savasana and sound healing experience to help you integrate and attain a state of peace and bliss.
Here are some of the benefits you might experience through Rebirthing Breathwork:
- Heal old emotional wounds and trauma
- Deep detox of your body
- Washes away tension, stress or blockages
- Boost your confidence and awareness
- Release old negative behavioral and patterns and subconscious belief systems
- Clear the energy system
- Activate the wisdom of your body for healing & transformation
- Insights and messages from the Divine
- Moving stuck energy, tension and stress in the body
- Increase vibrational levels in our system.
**We also offer couple’s sessions.**This is a beautiful modality to work with as a couple as you are deeply connected and can use your combined energies to both release personal intentions but also to work with the power of manifesting what you desire as a union.
