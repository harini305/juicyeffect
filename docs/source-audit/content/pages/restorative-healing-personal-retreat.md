## Section 2  `theme=white`
#### A private retreat for powerful healing and genuine rest
The Restorative Healing Retreat is an intimate, entirely one-to-one retreat for those who want to experience powerful energy healing while also giving themselves the time, privacy and space to genuinely rest.
This is not an intensive transformational retreat. There is no packed schedule and no expectation that you spend every day working deeply on yourself. Instead, personalised energy healing is woven into a beautiful, private and deeply restorative Bali experience.
You will have your own private villa, nourishing gluten-free and celiac-safe food, two private massages, time beside the rice fields, personalised healing sessions and generous periods of completely unstructured time.
**The philosophy - you do not have to choose between deep healing and deep rest. This retreat brings the two together — powerful healing work, followed by the space to receive, integrate and restore.**
#### Who is this retreat for?
This retreat is for someone who knows they need time away from everyday demands and would like something deeper than a conventional wellness break — but who is not looking for an intensive transformational retreat.
- You feel depleted, overstimulated or simply ready for a genuine pause.
- You are curious about energy healing or already value energetic work.
- You have done significant personal or spiritual work and want a more spacious experience this time.
- You are moving through a transition, period of change or demanding chapter of life.
- You want to be cared for rather than follow a demanding retreat schedule.
- You want privacy, beautiful surroundings, nourishing food and meaningful healing in one experience.
#### Your private retreat experience
The retreat is held entirely one-to-one. Sharon is your lead practitioner and overall guide throughout the experience, supported by carefully selected team members where their particular expertise adds value.
There is a clear framework, but the retreat is never rigid. Your sessions and practices are tailored according to your intentions, your needs and what emerges as the retreat unfolds.
#### What is included
- Private villa accommodation.
- Airport pick-up on arrival and private airport transfer on departure.
- Personalised one-to-one guidance with Sharon throughout the retreat.
- Shamanic Energy Healing.
- A personalised complementary healing session, selected from sound therapy, Reiki or breathwork according to your needs.
- A personalised Energetic Blueprint session based on Vedic Astrology.
- Personalised hypnotherapy.
- Two private massages in your villa.
- Nourishing gluten-free, celiac-safe meals from Unity Café.
- Organic coffee, plant-based milk options and fresh juices.
- Optional contemplative walks through the surrounding rice fields.
- Personal reflection and integration time.
- A carefully prepared welcome pack.
#### The Retreat Rhythm
The retreat has a carefully designed arc, but the experience remains spacious. The time between sessions is not empty time — it is part of the retreat. You may sleep, swim, read, journal, walk through the rice fields or do nothing at all.

#### A retreat that adapts to you
Although there is a carefully designed six-day framework, the retreat is never intended to feel like a fixed programme. Every guest arrives with different needs, experiences and intentions. The modalities, contemplative practices, depth of exploration and amount of structured time can therefore be adapted throughout the retreat.
The framework provides the container; you determine where the experience goes.
#### The private villa experience
Your villa is not simply accommodation — it is part of the retreat. It gives you privacy and space to sleep, swim, read, journal, rest, swim an integrate between sessions. Beautiful rice fields nearby provide an opportunity to walk in nature whenever it feels supportive.
#### Nourishment throughout your stay
Your breakfast and lunch are provided by Unity Café and are gluten-free and celiac-safe. Food is nourishing, beautiful and enjoyable, with organic coffee, plant-based milk options and fresh juices available. Meals are served to you in the privacy of your villa, allowing the relaxed rhythm of the retreat to continue.
**The essence of the experience - Come for the healing. Stay for the rest. Leave feeling restored.**
#### Your invitation
The Restorative Healing Retreat is an invitation to step away from the noise of everyday life, receive meaningful healing and give yourself something that is often surprisingly difficult to create at home: time, space and permission to truly rest.
