The story starts back around the year 2010, Sharon Marie was living in Australia and studying Ayurvedic Medicine with one of the most respected Ayurveda teachers on the planet.   Ayurveda is a 5000 year-old system of knowledge that teaches us that everyone has a unique and different body and mind constitution, what is referred to as your *prakruti*or *body-mind blueprint.*   This explains why many *wellbeing hacks* don't always work for everyone.  ​Rather than fixing symptoms, real solutions require addressing underlying root causes of concerns.
With a successful wellbeing practice already operating in Australia, a visit to Ubud in 2015 led to the idea of moving the business to Bali.  This dream became a reality in 2018 with the establishment of PT The Juicy Effect.

What we have discovered over the years is that there is no single technique or body of knowledge that perfectly suits everyone all of the time, simply because we are all unique. Shamanic Energy Healing, Vedic Astrology, Hypnotherapy for Inner Child Healing, Access Bars Energy Healing, Breathwork, Ayurveda, Kinesiology, Sound Healing and more.  **The value we provide** is that we have spent countless hours interpreting and integrating these various disciplines to get the best results.
