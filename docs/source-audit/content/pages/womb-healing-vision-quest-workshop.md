### About this workshop:
The womb has long been held as a place of mystery, creation, intuition and feminine wisdom.
This immersive day invites you to reconnect with the womb as an inner sanctuary — a place to listen deeply, explore what lives beneath the surface and reconnect with your own embodied knowing.
Whether you are new to womb work or already have a strong relationship with this sacred space, you are invited to explore the womb through somatic practice, meditation, ritual, ancestral exploration, deep listening and a powerful Womb Healing Vision Quest.
#### What we will explore
**The wisdom of the body** — Learning to slow down, become present and listen to the messages and sensations held within your body.
**The feminine lineage** — Exploring ancestral stories, patterns and experiences that may have shaped your relationship with your body, femininity and womb.
**Creativity & life-force** — Reconnecting with the generative energy of the womb — the part of us that creates, births, transforms and brings new life into being.
**Intuition & inner knowing** — Creating the space to hear the quieter wisdom beneath the noise of the everyday mind.
**Feminine sovereignty** — Exploring what it means to inhabit your body fully, trust your own knowing and reclaim your relationship with your feminine power.
### The Womb Healing Vision Quest
At the heart of the day is a guided **Womb Healing Vision Quest** — a sacred journey inward to connect with, embody and reclaim your womb space.
Through this experience, you will be invited to deepen your relationship with your womb, listen to her wisdom and explore what she may be ready to reveal, release or bring into your awareness.
**Lunch at Unity Café is included**, offering nourishing food and a gentle pause within the day. This creates space to rest, integrate and allow whatever has been opened through the morning to settle before continuing the journey.
### This Immersion May Be For You If…
- You feel called to deepen your relationship with your womb.
- You long for a deeper connection with your body and inner knowing.
- You sense there are stories, patterns or energies within your feminine lineage that are ready to be explored.
- You feel called to reconnect with your creativity, sensuality or life-force.
- Or perhaps you have simply felt a quiet inner calling from your womb — **a sense that she has something to say and you are ready to listen.**
