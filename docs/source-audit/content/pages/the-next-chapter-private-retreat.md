## Section 7  `theme=bright-inverse`
### FAQs
<!-- text-align: center -->
##### 1
<!-- text-align: right -->
##### Is this a retreat?
Not in the usual sense. The Next Chapter is private, personalised and designed around your needs. There are no group activities, fixed retreat timetable or requirement to share with strangers.
---
##### 2
<!-- text-align: right -->
##### Do I need to be spiritual?
No. Many guests are spiritually curious, but no particular belief system is required.  At The Juicy Effect, we combine the best from what works from a range of disciplines.
---
##### 3
<!-- text-align: right -->
##### Can couples attend?
Yes, depending on the situation. Couples may attend when both people are willing to participate honestly and respectfully, even if only person is part of the immersion.  A discovery call is required to assess suitability.
---
##### 4
<!-- text-align: right -->
##### Can this support burnout?
It may support people experiencing exhaustion, disconnection or transition-related overwhelm. However, it is not a medical or clinical treatment for burnout, trauma, depression, anxiety or any diagnosed condition.
---
##### 5.
<!-- text-align: right -->
##### What happens after I enquire?
You will be invited to complete a short pre-call questionnaire. If the experience appears suitable, we will schedule a private discovery call. From there, we create a personalised recommendation and proposal.
---
##### 6.
<!-- text-align: right -->
##### What makes this different?
Most retreats ask you to join a group and follow a shared schedule.  The Next Chapter is private, spacious and personalised.  There are no large circles, no forced  sharing, no rigid timetable, nopressure to have a dramatic breakthrough and no requirement to identify as spiritual.
Instead, you are supported by experienced practitioners in a calm, private environment where reflection, healing and integration can unfold at the right pace.
