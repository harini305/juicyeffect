#### A Structured Conversation for People at a Crossroads
There are times in life when the path ahead is no longer as clear as it once seemed.
You may be questioning your career, considering a major life change, navigating the end of a chapter, or simply sensing that something is asking for your attention.
From the outside, life may look successful.  From the inside, you may find yourself asking:
- What comes next?
- Is this still the life I want?
- What am I moving towards?
- What deserves my attention now?
- How do I make sense of what I'm feeling?
You do not need to have the answers.  You simply need a place to begin.  **Start Your Next Chapter** is a structured one-to-one conversation designed to help you step back, reflect, and gain greater clarity about where you are and what may come next.
#### Who Is This For?
This session is for people experiencing change, uncertainty, transition, or a growing sense that life is inviting them in a new direction.  You may be:
- Considering a career change or new professional direction
- Transitioning out of a leadership, executive, or business role
- Feeling successful on paper but uncertain about what comes next
- Navigating a significant life event or personal crossroads
- Exploring a different lifestyle, location, or way of working
- Feeling stuck between competing priorities or possibilities
- Looking for space to think without pressure, judgement, or advice
You do not need to be in crisis.
Many people seek clarity before making major decisions, while others simply recognise that something in their life deserves attention.
#### What can you expect?
Over approximately 90 minutes, we explore a specific sequence of questions to gain clarity about the things that are important to you:  Topics include:
- Where are you today?
- What is asking for attention?
- What may be emerging?
- What comes next?
#### What This Is Not
Start Your Next Chapter is not:
- Therapy or counselling
- Career coaching
- Business consulting
- Mentoring
- A personality assessment
- A process where someone tells you what to do
You remain the expert on your own life.  The purpose of this session is not to provide answers.  The purpose is to help you hear yourself more clearly, which is often what most people really need.
#### What You Will Receive
Following the session, you will receive a personalised Reflection Summary that captures:
- Key themes from the conversation
- Sources of energy and motivation that emerged
- Questions worth exploring further
- Possibilities that may deserve attention
- Suggested next steps or small experiments
This is not a report or assessment.
It is a reflection of the conversation and the insights that emerged.
